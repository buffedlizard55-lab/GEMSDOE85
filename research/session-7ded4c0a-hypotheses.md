## 3. Pre-Registered 3-Experiment Budget & Promotion Gate

Strict compliance with Parallel-Run Protocol §1–§6:
- **Evaluator:** `src/gems85/holdout.py` (`gems85-holdout-v1.0.0`, 4 spatially-blocked whole-segment folds `block_px=200, buffer_px=5, seed=20261022`, **60,988 withheld positives**, catalogue-derived quantities built strictly from `fold.visible`, visible faults masked pixel-exactly, pooled DTI $\alpha=0.2, \beta=0.8$, 300 m triangular kernel).
- **Experiments (total = 3, strictly within the 3-experiment / 2-hour cap):**
  1. **Exp 1 (`E1`):** Build and leakage-canary all catalogue-free channels (existing 30 + new `GAPVENT` channels including Rank 4 deconfounders and Rank 1 10m-3DEP/$\chi$/paleo-vent/2m-probe/dip-projected physics); train fold-proper `H85-GAPVENT` continuous 300 m kernel-target regressor and score at `37,654` dots (`sep=4.0, exclude_px=2`).
  2. **Exp 2 (`E2`):** `H85-SUBMOD300` — test exact 300 m lazy-greedy submodular marginal kernel-coverage allocation (`src/gems85/emitter.py` on calibrated sharpened probability $\hat{p}^\gamma$, $\gamma \in \{1.5, 2.5\}$) and `exclude_px=3` vs `exclude_px=2` against fixed-radius `poisson_thin` on `E1` at `37,654` dots.
  3. **Exp 3 (`E3`):** `H85-ORTHOFUSE` — test cross-mechanism fusion of `H85-GAPVENT` + unsupervised `GEO-Pb` crest + `CTXHGB-30` (`0.75 / 0.15 / 0.10` and rank-calibrated `0.70 / 0.30` with `GEO-Pb` crest) under the winning `E2` allocator at `37,654` dots.
- **Promotion Gate (ALL must hold to promote as primary candidate):**
  1. Pooled `HOLDOUT-DTI` @ `37,654` dots strictly beats `0.08082` (`S85-CTXFUS50`, current matched-budget holdout best) AND wins ≥ 3/4 folds vs `S85-CTXFUS50` and 4/4 folds vs `GEO-Pb crest` (`0.07475`).
  2. All single-feature and predicted-field leakage canaries have `AUC < 0.90`.
  3. Surface `|Spearman rho| <= 0.90` vs all 17 registry rasters AND raw 3-px dot overlap `<= 0.70` vs every comparable sparse/dotted submission (`<= 60,000` dots) in both the 17-raster registry and this repository's historical files (with dense space-filling prior overlaps disclosed transparently in `evidence/` and on the site).
  4. All 17 automated format checks in `src/gems85/submission.py::validate` + `vendor/gems57/gates.py::format_report` PASS on disk (`zeros` all-finite mode, `[0, 1]`, `EPSG:32611`, `3730×3292`, `float32`, no `nodata` tag, `0` mass within `2 px` of catalogue).
