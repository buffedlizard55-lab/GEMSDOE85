What this submission claims, and what it does not

No number on this site is a competition score. Every measurement here is a HOLDOUT-DTI: the official metric computed against withheld segments of the known catalogue on this machine. The live target is a private set of expert-mapped faults that are absent from that catalogue, so the holdout is a screen, not a prediction. Scores quoted for earlier GEMSDOE artifacts are OWNER-REPORT — copied from the operator's ledger, not re-verified against a DrivenData receipt here.

Session update 2026-10-22 — the primary above supersedes the GEO-Pb 90,000-dot file

What changed and why. The previous primary (GEO-Pb crest, 90,000 dots) was emitted at the budget that maximised the *catalogue holdout*, whose marginal credit bar (≈0.02/dot at DTI≈0.1) is far below the *live* bar (0.0588/dot at the family best 0.2778). The family's own live cascade (121k→0.1922, 44k→0.2600, 37,654→0.2778) shows the live operating point is ≈38k dots. The new primary is a different field (S85-CTXFUS: 30-channel contextual supervised detector rank-fused 50/50 with the GEO-Pb crest) emitted at that operating point, and it is the first field in this repo to beat the holdout best at matched budget (HOLDOUT-DTI 0.08082 vs 0.07475 @ 37,654, 3/4 folds, CIs overlap — see the run card). Three pre-registered alternatives tested in the same session (RELAY network-completion geometry, RELAY-gated crest, blurred-field emission) were negative and are published as such on the method page. The old files remain downloadable below, labelled superseded; none of them, and not the new file either, has an organizer score.

The other lanes in this repository

GEMSDOE85 was worked by independent lanes in parallel. The current primary is the S85-CTXFUS session candidate above. The GEO-Pb juxtaposition lane (H85-1) supplied the crest field that is one half of the fusion; its standalone files are the “previous GEO-Pb” alternates in the download list. The transtensional pull-apart + gravity worm duplex lane (H85-duplex) reached main first and its artifacts are kept alongside these ones, untouched:

  • Duplex lane — front page · its executive summary
  • Its submissions, also in docs/downloads/: duplex (42,000 dots) · TI-pure (38,000) · worm-pure (46,000)

The two lanes' holdout numbers are not comparable. They use different evaluators, different fold geometry and different withheld-positive counts, so the duplex lane's reported 0.1542 and this lane's 0.09838 cannot be ranked against each other. Doing so would be exactly the apples-to-oranges error this repository's instrument calibration was built to catch. Both lanes' artifacts are published; neither has an organizer score. The two lanes do now share one metric implementation, and tests/test_metric.py asserts the fast O(29N) path equals a literal brute-force transcription of the official definition — so at least the ruler is common.
