0. Current primary — S85-CTXFUS (session 2026-10-22, arena/22847afd)

Hypothesis (pre-registered in research/session-22847afd-hypotheses.md before measuring): a fault is a line in context, not a pixel. The shipped field rank-fuses (50/50) two mechanisms that fail differently: (a) a contextual supervised detector — HistGradientBoosting on the 24 catalogue-free channels below plus six line-context channels (1.5 km along-strike line support, multi-scale context means, five-field corroboration count), trained per fold on the visible catalogue only; and (b) the GEO-Pb crest of section 1. Emission is the measured gate configuration: 37,654 binary dots, 4 px minimum separation, zero mass within 2 px of the catalogue. Named mimic: erosional volcanic mesa/flow margins.

Result (HOLDOUT-DTI, evaluator gems85-holdout-v1.0.0, 60,988 withheld positives): the fusion is the only arm that beat the previous holdout best (GEO-Pb crest 0.07475 @ 37,654) — at both separations and both budgets, 3/4 folds positive, with overlapping 95% CIs. 1. The lane — GEO-Pb, a juxtaposition detector

Every fault detector in the 60 sibling GEMSDOE repositories is a derivative operator — Sato/Frangi/Hessian ridges, tilt angle, horizontal gradient, Euler deconvolution, worms, gradient coherence. A derivative asks “is this field changing fast here?”. A fault, physically, is a juxtaposition: it puts two different geological domains side by side. The right question is the two-sample one — “do the rocks on the two sides of this line have different joint geophysical signatures?”

GEO-Pb answers it directly. At each pixel and each of eight candidate strikes it forms the offset difference of a covariance-whitened 10-band stack across the candidate line (a windowed Hotelling-style step statistic — the geophysical analogue of the half-disc Pb boundary detector from computer vision), takes the median of three independent physics domains (potential-field/subsurface, airborne radiometric, topographic) so at least two must agree, and multiplies by an along-strike persistence term over 1.5 km so only straight, continuing boundaries survive.

Why it should find a fault the catalogue lacks. The supplied labels are USGS Quaternary faults plus INGENIOUS — structures mapped from surface evidence. A fault concealed under Holocene alluvium with no scarp still juxtaposes different basement susceptibility, different cover thickness and different surface geochemistry. Each band changes a little; the joint 10-vector changes a lot. That is exactly the class a scarp-first catalogue under-samples and a derivative detector under-weights.

Named non-fault processes that mimic it (declared before measuring): distal alluvial-fan margins and Lake Lahontan highstand shorelines (lobate and closed — killed by the straightness term); aeromagnetic/radiometric acquisition seams (single-domain — killed by the two-of-three vote, and audited below); erosional volcanic mesa and flow margins (straight and multi-domain — the irreducible confounder, declared, not solved).

2. The instrument — hide-and-recover, spatially blocked

The catalogue is split into connected fault segments; segments are assigned to four folds by spatial block so a whole region is hidden at once. Each catalogue pixel within 5 px of a withheld pixel is also removed from visible. Every catalogue-derived quantity is recomputed from visible only; the prediction is masked to zero pixel-exactly on visible; the fold is scored with the official metric against the withheld segments; the four folds are pooled into one DTI.

IR-85-001 — a shared-tooling defect found and fixed. A hide-and-recover holdout cannot score a finished raster that was built with a hard catalogue exclusion: the artifact has already deleted every dot near the segments the holdout then hides, so its score is forced to ≈ 0 by its own success condition. Measured here: the family's best live artifact (0.2778 OWNER-REPORT) scores 0.0048 on this holdout, while Hedge-v2, which puts 60,988 dots directly on the catalogue, scores 0.3177 and is worth 0.1563 live. The fix is structural: holdout.score_folds takes a callable and re-executes the rule per fold. Every sibling repository that correlated a static-artifact holdout against the live board was measuring this defect, not an instrument.
