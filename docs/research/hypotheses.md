### 1.2 Five Fundamental Bottlenecks Holding `H33` Back Below `0.3195–0.4413`
1. **Discovery `IR-85-LIDAR-NORMAL-90DEG` — 90° Structure-Tensor Orientation Bug in 1 m LiDAR Band 11 (`"strike"`):**
   - Across the entire repository lineage, `lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif` Band 11 (`"strike"`) was assumed to encode fault strike azimuth ($\approx \text{N}015^\circ\text{E}$ across Basin-and-Range normal faults).
   - Direct pixel-level verification against `289,141` mapped Quaternary fault cells proves that Band 11 actually peaks on real faults at **`110°–130°` (`1.166×` enrichment)** and bottoms out at **`0°–20°` (`0.823×` depletion; `cos²(Band11 - 20°) AUC = 0.4702 < 0.50` vs `cos²(Band11 - 110°) AUC = 0.5143`)** because $\frac{1}{2}\text{atan2}(2J_{xy}, J_{xx}-J_{yy})$ measures the **principal elevation gradient / dip normal vector** $\hat{\mathbf{n}}_{1\text{m}} \parallel \mathbf{S}_{h\min}$ (`90°` rotated from strike)!
2. **Blind Concealed Subsurface Basins (`90%+` Topographic Weight vs `<10%` Subsurface/Geothermal Weight in `H33`):**
   - Playas, eolian sand sheets, Lake Lahontan lacustrine silts, and young Holocene fan aprons completely bury blind active geothermal faults (the primary targets of DOE INGENIOUS!). `H33` ignored **polar 3D hanging-wall dipole sign coherence** (`+grad(basement) . u_dip > 0` AND `-grad(gravity) . u_dip > 0`), **Gallardo & Meju (2004) cross-gradient coupling** (`G49 = 0.2376` live), **FFT upward-continuation depth persistence**, and **de-aliased ($\sigma=36\text{ px}$) magnetotelluric smectite boundaries**.

   - In `USGS_gbHeatFlowWells_wEstimates.shp` (`1,546` unique in-footprint boreholes; `data/external/usgs_heat_flow_wells_epsg32611.csv`), **993 boreholes exhibit positive convective thermal excess (`hf_resid > +20 mW/m²`, up to `+103.8 mW/m²` at Dixie Valley)** where hot fluids ascend along permeable unmapped fault conduits. Neither `H33` nor any scored raster coupled borehole convective heat flow + DOE GDR #1391 silica geothermometer cooling disequilibrium + 2 m shallow thermal probes + siliceous sinter vents with **Out-of-Quadrant Morris–Ferrill–Henderson (1996) 3D Dilation Tendency**.

### Named Non-Fault Geological & Anthropogenic Mimics + Explicit Mitigations
1. **Lake Lahontan / Bonneville Wave-Cut Pleistocene Shorelines (`~1,330 m` elevation contour):** Follow elevation contours horizontally with crisp 1–3 m steps, mimicking contour-parallel scarps. *Mitigation:* Wave-cut shorelines follow closed zero-dip elevation contours around basin margins with **zero** subsurface gravity/basement hanging-wall dipole (`d3_hw_triple_concordance == 0`), **zero** magnetic edge persistence (`d3_fft_up500_persist == 0`), and all azimuths around $360^\circ$ rather than perpendicular to $\theta_{S_{h\min}}$.
2. **Alluvial Fan Incised Distributary Gullies & Bedrock Canyon Walls:** Sharp V-shaped cross-profiles perpendicular to range fronts. *Mitigation:* Suppressed by `d1_lid_tect_vs_fluv` (`cross_max` denominator), `90°`-corrected contour alignment (`cos < 0` on down-fan gullies), relief normalization (`piedmont_step`), and Dual-Expert slope de-confounding (`Expert A` excludes raw slope).
3. **Shallow Cenozoic Basalt Flow Margins, Caldera Ring Dikes & Mesozoic Pluton Contacts:** Produce strong shallow aeromagnetic/gravity edges without Quaternary slip. *Mitigation:* Separated via FFT upward-continuation depth persistence (`500 m` vs `100 m`), curvilinear anisotropy (`line / iso` rejects circular plutons/calderas), and Terzaghi–Morris extensional azimuth coupling.
4. **Playa Highway / Railroad Berms (`US-50`, `US-95`, `I-80`):** Raised 1–2 m linear embankments across flat playas mimic micro-scarps in 1 m LiDAR. *Mitigation:* Per `IR-85-ROAD-01` (`17.14%` of real range-front faults lie $\le 100\text{ m}$ from roads!), we **never** hard-mask roads; instead `d5_road_valley_berm_flag` selectively penalizes road-proximate steps ONLY on flat (`slope < 1.8°`), low-gravity-gradient valley floors.
